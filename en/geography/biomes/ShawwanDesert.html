Shawwan Desert

The burning sands of the Shawwaan desert spread over the majority of Osteros's northmost territory. Its dunes, windswept by the hot gases of the sun, hosts a myriad of ruins, remanents of the civilisations that weren't prepared to face its trials. Everyone adventuring through the desert shall distruss the calm, even more near the giant Oaku river, a true paradise for the thirsty ones which make it a perfect hunting ground for the local fauna.

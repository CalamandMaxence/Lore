Turia

Turia is a central kingdom of Atelesia, occupying the isthmus that links Osteros to Esteros. Its geographic position makes it a major crossroads of trade and diplomacy between the two continents. Extending its influence through an advanced fleet, Turia has become an ally of the elven lands that occupy the Entremonde.

History

Shortly after the collapse of the Empire of Dolomné, the tribes on the empire's fringes seized the moment to spread chaos. It was then that Arthur Turia emerged, a charismatic strategist and great orator, who unified the clans left abandoned by the fallen empire. Proclaimed Arthur I in the year 72 of the central calendar, he founded the kingdom of Turia around the city of Penterony.
Turia expanded gradually, securing the isthmus and stabilizing its borders in the Entremonde. Great works campaigns were launched: roads, forges and mines, ports and defensive strongholds. The first royal high-seas fleet was inaugurated in 216, and Turia became a naval power living off the trade between the continents. The capital moved, logically, to Nevhilbor that same year.
The rise of the Empire of Stellata, to the west of the kingdom, marked a turning point in Turia's history. Two wars, triggered by the expansion of the Astral Giant, ravaged western Turia: a first from 341 to 357, which forced the kingdom to give up the domain of Watchsburry, and a second from 410 to 443. Since that period Turia has taken a strong interest in the applications of magic, a leaning reinforced by the affair of the Spy Paladins in 487. That affair, born of Regalican propaganda during the post-war instability, soured the ties between Regalica and Turia and halted the attempted alliance between Turia and the elven lands. The Elf-Human Alliance of the Entremonde was sealed nonetheless in 538 of the Turian calendar, establishing military and academic cooperation with the Chancellery of Eebel and the United Tribes of Naandedil.

Institutions

Turia rests on a hereditary monarchy. The king embodies the unity of the kingdom and is the guarantor of its values. At present, Elvyn Turia, a descendant of Arthur I, reigns from Nevhilbor.
The corps of Royal Paladins is an elite order charged with the safety of the royal family. Seven at most, these men and women at arms are kept anonymous to the public.
A judicial order, the Guardians of the Values, upholds the founding principles throughout the kingdom, secularism among them. Even Nerialism is accepted here, despite the tensions with Regalica.

Faith

Unlike many nations of Atelesia, Turia is secular.

Religion is a tool that dispenses fervor and ethics, but in the wrong hands it breeds only bitterness and envy.
Follow the Values of the kingdom, and you will tell ethics apart from manipulation. — Arthur Turia

Turian identity rests on the Values, a set of precepts taught from childhood and carved into the stone of many buildings. They include keeping one's given word, courage in the face of injustice, impartial justice, and so on. The Guardians of the Values see to it that no divine or ecclesiastical authority sets itself above the law.

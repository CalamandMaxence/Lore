Stars

The Moons

In the night sky of Atelesia, two moons dance among the distant stars: Mar and Ebe. The second, a dark grey, is larger than the first, but because of its distance it often appears smaller than Mar, and is depicted that way. Their orbits are slightly tilted against the path of the sun, which makes eclipses rare and turns them into omens of great change.

The Rift of Worlds

At night, a vast loop of reddish stars called the Rift of Worlds curves across the sky, crossing the path of Mar twice. On its edge, where it meets that path, shines a cluster of brighter stars: the Divine. It is said that these lights come from a multitude of other worlds.
